# Story route from the editable world

Status: accepted projection used by the complete first-construction manuscript.
It selects events from `WORLD-MODEL.md`; it does not add world facts.

Voice is rendered from each person's current life trajectory and local Cuts.
Sensory detail is rendered first from participating Things, their activity, the
characters' presence, and the containing place's current Events. A place Event
is opened separately only when it changes access, timing, choice, or consequence.

The book is twelve compact passages, approximately 9,000--11,000 words. Its
rendered order is not the construction order. It opens with the returned table
because that is the point at which the distinction between a correct mechanism
and a reliable computational institution becomes dramatic, then returns to the
small 1843 choice that made the whole history possible.

## 1. The Returned Table — May 1854

Events: E23--E25. Focal route: Halden, then Davies.

A sealed packet returns to the Calculation Office. The printed table is wrong,
but the apparatus's trace is internally exact. Halden follows the error backward
from output to card to a transposed source number, then discovers that the
promised duplicate check bears no second signature. The opening question is not
whether the machine failed, but who will admit what the machine was part of.

Change: the apparent technical failure becomes a governance and capacity
failure. End on Halden finding his own delivery authorization.

## 2. Thirty Days — August 1843

Events: E01. Focal route: Babbage and Lovelace through letters and one meeting.

Eleven years earlier, Lovelace's proposal reaches Babbage after government
withdrawal and the Clement rupture have taught him to fear finite obligations.
She expects either refusal or a struggle over control. He offers neither
acceptance nor rupture: thirty days to discover whether any bounded undertaking
can preserve the larger architecture. The choice is small enough to believe and
large enough to branch history.

Change: a relationship that was about interpretation becomes a negotiation
about authority.

## 3. The Estimator — September 1843

Events: E02--E03. Focal route: Halden.

George Farrow, known to Halden through instrument and railway supply and to
Babbage through the former Clement network, sends Halden a packet no sensible
estimator would price.
He needs the £25 fee and distrusts visions that make refusal morally impossible.
Instead of quoting the Engine, he lists everything that has no accepted answer:
interfaces, quantities, gauges, suppliers, tests, payment stops. Attempts to
separate one machine part keep reopening dependencies. His creative reversal is
to reorganize the eleven-pound packet by answerable claim instead. Babbage,
Lovelace, and Halden then work the first finite arrangement into a smaller
object that can carry one architectural claim without pretending to be the
architecture. Their different forms of intelligence briefly compose rather
than merely negotiate.

Change: the third founder is earned through an act of refusal and decomposition,
not introduced as a ready-made savior.

## 4. What Can Be Bought — winter 1843–44

Events: E04--E05. Focal route: Lovelace, Davies, and the traveling reports.

Guardian will not buy an Engine; Davies can name twelve recurrence cases whose
answers can be computed independently. Whitworth will not bless a vision; his
works can say which reduced interfaces admit gauges and which tolerances are
unsupported. Davies's marked copy of Lovelace's Notes makes clear that his
refusal is discipline exercised against interest, not indifference. Lovelace
turns the two refusals into the exact question the undertaking can answer.

Change: customer and reviewer narrow the claim without joining the founding
relationship.

## 5. The Finite Compact — April 1844

Events: E06--E07. Focal route: all three principals.

The principals assign authority line by line. Babbage keeps architecture;
Lovelace keeps examples, comparison, and wording of the claim; Halden keeps
price, schedule, and consequences of accepted change. Manufacture, customer
acceptance, and cash custody remain outside all three. The difficult clause is
the successor register: a brilliant improvement may be true and still not enter
the machine now being paid for. When the solicitor offers a deputy for
Lovelace's signature, she crosses out the blank rather than let an unspecified
substitute hollow out the authority she has just won. The choice is defensible
and leaves her own function dependent upon one carrier.

Let the hesitation and the solicitor's glance towards Babbage carry her fear
of replacement. Babbage does not nominate a deputy; she removes the provision
without anyone resolving whether that protects the undertaking or only her
place in it.

Change: complementary competence becomes an institution. End when Babbage signs
the boundary he will later want to exceed.

## 6. Parts That Will Not Become a Machine — July 1846

Events: E08--E10. Focal route: works floor, Babbage, then Lovelace's correction
record.

Two assemblies meet their drawings and bind when joined. Babbage offers a
redesign that would improve more than the faulty interface. Halden refuses to
move the accepted object; Farrow supports his foreman's finding despite the
delay to his next payment; Lovelace insists that both the rejected drawing and
the reason for refusal survive. Re-machining feels like waste until the
composed assembly finally turns. Foreman, estimator, and inventor register the
release through load, paid interface, and architecture, and the recovery is
allowed to feel pleasurable before it is entered.

Change: the conditions prove useful under negative pressure and each principal's
form of intelligence becomes visible.

## 7. The Call — 1847–48

Events: E11. Focal route: Halden and the subscriber ledger.

Credit tightens before a subscription stage. Halden can preserve appearances by
ordering work without cash, enlarge the promise to attract money, or slow the
build after paying already earned wages. He chooses delay. Babbage experiences
lost time as intellectual danger; Halden experiences unpaid work as a concrete
breach. The machine survives because nothing spectacular happens.

Change: restraint consolidates before later success tests whether it is truly
stable.

## 8. The Sealed Cases — May 1851

Events: E12--E15. Focal route moves among all three without merging their
readings.

The packet was closed before the result. The apparatus executes two runs over
twelve cases and agrees with the independent comparison. The passage gives the
success room to feel enormous while keeping the public sentence narrow.
Babbage hears vindication, Halden hears the birth of a profession, and Lovelace
hears evidence that their conditions worked. Their claims already differ, but
the difference does not cancel their shared pleasure. The second run is allowed
to unfold card by card through the objects and practices each participant can
read: operations, trace, bearings, time, seals, and comparison. Davies briefly
wants to inspect a failed clearing himself, then submits his curiosity to the
prospective stop he helped require.

Change: a positive shock begins three different adaptations. End with a few
unnecessary empty cycles of the reader: Babbage wants to hear them, Lovelace
laughs because she wanted the same thing, and Halden knowingly allows unpriced
motion. The inquiry opens the next section instead of interrupting the
achievement here.

## 9. Four Deposits — May 1851–April 1852

Events: E16--E19. Focal route: Halden's capacity ledger and Lovelace's dissent.

The first inquiry arrived while the trial room was being cleared; publication
brings three more. The office can independently check two jobs in a quarter,
but four offices offer deposits for final delivery in April--June 1854. Early
specimen work can be spread out. Customers close their accounts at the end of
1853 and reconcile them through March 1854; final signed returns are released
no earlier than 1 April. State that dependency briefly so neither the long lead
time nor the first quarter supplies final checking capacity for these packets.
Babbage argues that visible use will draw
resources; Halden argues that deposits are the resources. Their agreement feels
independent to each of them although both judgments have been moved by the same
success and response. Neale has begun teaching Wills, but he refuses to count
supervised practice as a second independent checker. Babbage and Halden appeal
to one another's expertise, exposing their common confidence to the reader;
Lovelace confines her objection to the checking capacity she can demonstrate
and does not diagnose the motive beneath their agreement.
Babbage urges four jobs and Halden, holding contracting authority, accepts them
in February. In April, after the preparations begin to overlap, Lovelace writes
and signs the arithmetic of preparation and checking beside the arithmetic of
the machine rather than letting their agreement erase contrary evidence.

Change: the conditions remain legible but cease to govern all action.

## 10. The Unfunded Successor — August–November 1852

Events: E20--E22. Focal route: Lovelace from Ockham through documents; then the
two surviving principals receiving the news.

Lovelace meets the consequence of her own sole-signature rule in the marked
copy. She asks William whether he would have signed, then admits she would not
have let him. He offers neither a verdict nor an easy substitute. The
unsettled exchange precedes her narrower request: who will
perform independent comparison, with authority to refuse delivery, when Neale
cannot? Her signed qualification carries her claim boundary; she does not ask
for a successor to her title. Training a second checker would delay deliveries
and consume cash; the answer is deferred. When Babbage brings a self-comparison
design, their old collaborative speed briefly returns. Lovelace distinguishes
an unforeseen consequence from machine intention, and Babbage narrows
verification to internal comparison. Guardian's source sheet---not a further
diagnosis---shows the remaining boundary. They defer the human succession. Her
death then terminates an active pressure the others knowingly failed to
replace.

Change: anticipated loss becomes unprepared loss. The relationship ends in the
world and continues differently inside each survivor's understanding. A short
bridge shows the four jobs overlapping through 1853 as the office adapts to
excess by normalizing local exceptions. Guardian's final signed return arrives
on 1 April 1854 and replaces its provisional source; none of the four final
signed returns was available in the preceding quarter. Neale puts aside the
old checking marks. The physical distinction between the two source sheets
carries the scheduling constraint into the opening's omitted check without
another explanatory speech. The other three release dates remain unresolved
within April.

## 11. The Seller's Conditions — May 1854–March 1855

Events: E26--E27, returning to the opening. Focal route: Halden and Babbage.

Halden discloses the trace, refunds Guardian, corrects the table, and reconciles
the cash. Babbage argues that wrong input cannot impeach a correct mechanism;
Halden answers that the office sold a table, not a private mechanism trace. The
accounts cannot support another quarter. During correction Neale gives Wills
the active reading place and later puts his own name beneath their paired work.
Halden records his own two authorizing acts—the four jobs and the omitted
check—and closes rather than sell another promise.

Change: Halden's ledger intelligence turns on himself. Babbage retains the error
record even while resisting its interpretation.

## 12. The Complete Record — 1871

Events: E28--E30. Focal route: Babbage and Henry; short 1900 coda.

Babbage prepares custody near the end of his life. Plans and the successful test
would form a cleaner inheritance. The returned table, Lovelace qualification,
and closure account would make it complete. He chooses complete custody without
claiming that this repairs the failed institution or completes the Engine.

Change: his final act changes the meaning of preservation. The coda opens the
packet in 1900 and asserts only that it survived.

## First route-level revision

Opening the world into scenes exposed no fatal causal contradiction. It did
expose two risks, both repaired in this route:

1. **Halden could look like an infallible manager.** The story therefore places
   his own expansion decision and omitted-check authorization at the center of the
   failure.
2. **Lovelace's death could be made to cause the error.** The capacity shortage
   and rejected succession cost now occur before her death; her death removes a
   resisting agent but does not make the later decision.

The middle is not a parade of technical incidents: the 1846 binding assembly
tests scope, the 1847 call tests money, the 1851 trial tests evidence, and the
1852 deposits test whether the same discipline survives success. Each prepares
the later failure in a different domain.
