# Selection of the complementary venture principal

Status: role, identity, biography, and access route accepted for this
construction.

The new construction requires a third complete principal because the first
candidate round could not explain how continued negotiation became priced work,
staged obligations, and an accountable organization. It does **not** restore an
earlier character.

## Selected occupational role

The principal begins as a London engineering estimator and works agent. His
ordinary competence is reading drawings as obligations: bills of quantities,
supplier dependencies, inspection stages, payment stops, schedule risk, and
accepted changes. He may later become secretary and superintendent of contracts
for the undertaking, but only after a bounded feasibility account earns that
authority.

His first plot-causal act occurs after the August 1843 divergence. Babbage pays
for a limited initial estimate from the private means already documented as
supporting his assistants and design work; the record does not infer a liquid
construction budget from that fact. George Farrow knows Halden through
instrument and railway supply and Babbage through the former Clement network.
Farrow forwards the inquiry, and Halden accepts a four-week report for twenty-
five pounds with the right to recommend that nothing be built. Farrow's later
manufacturing bid is admitted only after Whitworth's works has independently
reviewed the reduced interfaces.

## Authority boundary

- Babbage retains architecture and chief design judgment.
- Lovelace contributes procedure, exposition, evidential conditions, and
  negotiated governance; she does not command works or capital.
- The venture founder coordinates estimates, contracts, schedules, staged
  payments, and the consequences of acceptance.
- An independent works owns manufacture and inspection evidence.
- A customer owns its input assumptions, acceptance, commercial use, and the
  decision to cancel.
- Investors or trustees retain custody of capital.
- A later custodian receives documents and relics; the founder does not inherit
  succession automatically.

The founder has no architectural authorship, customer authority, workshop
ownership, personal financing magic, or general veto. His veto is limited to a
change in the accepted production configuration that alters cost, schedule,
interface, or test. Babbage may pursue such a change in the separate successor
register.

## Human arc to test rather than assume

The founder wants to complete one honest bounded undertaking, support his
household, and establish that organizing difficult work is itself consequential
work. His strength is finite framing and contractual memory. His defensive
proxy is the growing wish to be recognized as the man who made the impossible
deliverable. After the trial, inquiries and deposits may therefore perturb him
as strongly as they perturb Babbage. The crucial candidate question is whether
he relaxes his own stages and becomes jointly responsible for overcommitment.
If he remains an infallible adult who merely restrains Babbage, the character
and the world must be revised.

His possible growth is equally bounded: after the error, does he preserve a
false appearance of solvency, blame the technical side, or publish the complete
ledger and close? No outcome is evidence of a universal trait. It is one
decision under one set of information and obligations.

## Why the principal is fictional

The researched historical people presently support narrower roles. Whitworth
is a plausible reviewer of gauges, tolerances, and a bounded commission, not an
established founder or financier. Griffith Davies belongs on the customer side.
William King-Noel constrains Lovelace's household, legal, and access routes.
Joseph Clement's rupture supplies the warning and he dies in 1844. Assigning the
whole new function to any of them would require more distortion than one
explicitly fictional, period-compatible works agent.

The accepted principal is Edward Halden, born in London in 1803: a fictional
commercial clerk, quantity estimator, and works agent with a wife, two children,
approximately two hundred and thirty pounds in savings at the divergence, and
no workshop or inherited fortune. His pre-1843 lifecycle establishes trade
formation, household, income dependence, and work experience. It does not
cause, participate in, or rewrite any documentary-prefix Event; every such
record is marked authored fictional background compatible with the prefix.
