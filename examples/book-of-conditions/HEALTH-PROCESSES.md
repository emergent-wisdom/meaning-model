# Opened health processes for the principals

Health is an optional Event-process beneath `body` in the loaded person
template. When the process is opened, one local Cut asks how one unit of the
person's current bodily health condition is divided among `healthy functioning`,
`illness burden`, and an uncertainty remainder. The children are complementary
under that question and sum to one. A `.50/.50/.00` row is an intermediate
condition; `.95/.03/.02` is close to the run's healthy reference. This is a
relative description inside the model, not a clinical diagnosis.

## Coarse health-condition Cuts

These values are deliberately broad parents. Later illness and recovery Events
may refine them, and a medically relevant fact such as a date, symptom, or
treatment remains ordinary world data rather than a Cut weight.

## Charles Babbage

The health process begins with recurrent childhood illness that interrupts
ordinary schooling and increases dependence on private education. Adult bodily
capacity supports sustained mathematical and mechanical work across long
periods; bereavement changes his inner life without being redescribed as a
medical disease. During the counterfactual build, health is not used to cause
his decisions or excuse overcommitment. Aging becomes materially relevant after
1855 as travel and physical work narrow, and terminates agency at his death in
1871. The custody decision occurs while this terminal horizon is foreseeable.

Shape: early instability → long stable capacity → gradual age-related drift →
anticipated terminal transition.

## Ada Lovelace

The health process includes recurrent childhood fragility, a serious adolescent
illness followed by prolonged recovery, adult periods in which bodily limits
compete with study, childbirth, household life, and correspondence, and a final
progressive illness in 1851--52. The story does not convert illness into
intellectual weakness. It changes presence, available hours, travel, reliance
on documents, and the urgency of succession. By August 1852 the loss of active
capacity is anticipated; Babbage supports delay and Halden defers a funded
replacement over her objection.
Her move from Ockham to Great Cumberland Place and death in November form the
terminal transition.

Shape: recurrent shocks and recovery → constrained productive baseline →
progressive decline without recovery → anticipated terminal transition.

## Edward Halden

Halden has an ordinary working bodily trajectory: childhood growth, sustained
adult capacity for office and site work, fatigue during the venture's busiest
periods, and gradual aging after closure. No illness causes the four-job
decision, the omitted check, or the office failure. That absence is deliberate:
his decisive mistakes arise from status, means, partnership, and judgment. He
returns to salaried work after 1855 and dies in 1874 after ordinary late-life
decline, which remains unopened unless the final book later needs it.

Shape: growth → long stable capacity with temporary fatigue → gradual aging →
terminal transition.
