# Construction task for the independent rewrite

The task below was the human-supplied starting premise. In the run it was read
as `PROMPT.md`, with the historical package as `ERA-PACKAGE.md`. The public copy
of that package is [HISTORICAL-SOURCES.md](HISTORICAL-SOURCES.md).

Write The Book of Conditions with the Meaning Model: a novel in twelve chapters.

The era package, ERA-PACKAGE.md in this folder, is the research the book starts from: what was true in Britain up to
August 1843 about Charles Babbage's engines and the people around them. Real history holds up to then.

Then one thing goes differently. On 14 August 1843, Ada Lovelace offers to conduct the business of the Analytical
Engine so that Babbage's undivided energies can go to the work. Historically he refused all her conditions. In this
book he does not refuse: he goes on negotiating with her.

Everything after that is yours to build: who else is drawn in, what they make together, what happens to the Engine and
to them, and how it ends. Keep the real lives the change would not touch as they were, and where you change one, say
why.

Build the world beneath the story first, every person as a whole life, and let the story come from the model. Use Sema
for patterns and reasoning as you build. I want to watch the model fill in the live viewer.

## Continuation and feedback

The human authorized continuation and then a deeper pass through the world and
prose. Three fresh Claude Sonnet 5.5 instances, with no tools and no access to the
older book, supplied editorial criticism. The writing agent revised the model
and chapters in response. A final instruction corrected reviewer identities
without changing the prose. The human did not supply replacement passages.
The agent generated the literary text and the model; this does not mean the
work had no human premise, supplied research, supervision or stopping decisions.
