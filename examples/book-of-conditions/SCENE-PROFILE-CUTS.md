# Selected scene-profile Cuts

Status: accepted authored interpretations for the first construction.

The nine `IS` processes of a life coexist and do not sum. Numbers appear here
only where the loaded person template asks how one declared unit is divided
among mutually exclusive sibling answers. Every row or bracketed triplet sums
to one and includes a remainder. These Cuts make the principals' changing
inner organization visible without assigning a score to a person.

### How WHAT relates to outlook and FEELS

These profiles answer different questions and therefore have different
denominators. The slow fulfillment-outlook Cut in `PERSON-MODELS.md` divides a
period's represented outlook among assured fulfillment, threatened
fulfillment, and unresolved remainder. If its threat child is opened, the
threat-character Cut divides that threat among the five core wanted regions.
WHAT instead divides all motivational attention in one scene among those
regions. FEELS divides the scene's emotional attention among emotions. Thus a
person may represent substantial threat without fear dominating FEELS, because
the same appraisal can organize anger, sadness, or action; and a dominant WHAT
concern need not be the concern most threatened. The threat child may be
multiplied by its own conditional children because that is one hierarchical
opening. Neither WHAT nor FEELS is multiplied into it unless a separate joint
Cut is explicitly declared.

The numbers do not dictate sentences. They constrain the reading. High local
assurance permits longer projection into what follows; high threat turns speech
toward boundaries, losses, and immediate choices. Character determines the
form: Babbage protects distinctions in the architecture, Lovelace writes
conditions that can survive her absence, and Halden itemizes what the office
can pay for or prove. FEELS still determines whether the same threat is carried
mainly as fear, anger, sadness, or another appraisal.

## Health is a separate opening

`HEALTH-PROCESSES.md` contains the two-pole health-condition Cut beneath
`body`. It divides the current condition between healthy functioning, illness
burden, and remainder. It is not mixed into WHAT, HOW, or FEELS.
