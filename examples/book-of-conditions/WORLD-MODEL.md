# The Book of Conditions: editable world model

Status: accepted authored counterfactual world for the first construction.

This is the working Meaning Model for the story. It is intentionally readable
and editable. It exists to help construct a plausible world and an interesting
book, not to turn authorship into a compliance exercise. Documentary events
through 14 August 1843 come from the frozen prefix. Everything after the
divergence is an explicit counterfactual choice unless separately sourced.

## The whole world in one paragraph

After the government abandons the Difference Engine, Charles Babbage nearly
repeats the same open-ended pattern with the Analytical Engine, but in August
1843 he continues negotiating with Ada Lovelace rather than ending their
partnership. Their negotiation exposes a missing function: neither can price,
stage, and accept commercial responsibility for a finite build. George Farrow,
known both to Babbage's former workshop network and to railway suppliers,
introduces Edward Halden, a fictional London engineering
estimator, who will undertake only a paid feasibility study. Babbage supplies
the architecture, Lovelace the operational and evidential conditions, Halden
the contracts and stages, an independent works the manufacture, and Guardian
Assurance a bounded customer question. They complete and independently test a
small card-directed calculating apparatus in 1851. All three experience its
narrow success as a genuine shared achievement even while they interpret what
it demonstrates differently. Commercial attention then helps that positive
shock undo their earlier discipline:
Babbage reads it as vindication of the general architecture and Halden reads
inquiries as proof of a viable service, while Lovelace insists that neither
follows. She also recognizes a defect in her own
earlier design: in protecting a rare authority by tying it to her signature,
she left its continuity dependent upon one person. The two men open a
calculation office beyond its known checking capacity and defer the independent
checking succession she then demands. After Lovelace dies, a manual input error
passes an omitted duplicate check; the mechanism calculates correctly from the wrong input, a customer
returns the table, and the office closes in 1855. Babbage eventually preserves
the successful test, the warning, the error, and the accounts together. The
counterfactual therefore produces no Engine Age: it produces one real bounded
proof and one unusually complete record of why technical truth did not become a
reliable institution.

## Roots and hierarchy

- **Universe** is the root Thing.
- **History** is the Universe's lifecycle Event.
- **The bounded Engine undertaking, 18 August 1843--1871** is the parent Event
  of the accepted counterfactual and is contained beneath History.
- Its periods are disjoint temporal children. Their shares are derived from
  their durations and therefore sum to one; no author supplies those weights.
- Engine design, manufacture, the calculation office, the founding
  relationships, and public response are overlapping processes. They describe
  the same history from different questions and do **not** sum.
- Events inside a period are happenings linked to the period. They become a
  Cut only when a named question divides a real unit over them.

## Principal Things and lives

### Charles Babbage

Historical person. His documentary life and work before the divergence remain
unchanged. In the counterfactual he retains authority over architecture, but
not over the works' acceptance evidence, customer acceptance, or venture cash.
His strength and failure use the same capacity: he sees a succession of more
general designs. Under material constraints this produces invention; under
contractual constraints it tempts him to treat a finite success as merely the
first installment of the larger thing.

His growth is conditional rather than steady: he accepts a bounded obligation
in 1844, regresses after recognition in 1851, initially calls the 1854 failure
external to the machine, and finally preserves the entire human-machine record
instead of plans alone.

### Ada Lovelace

Historical person. Her counterfactual authority is deliberately bounded. She
defines operational examples, disclosure conditions, the comparison protocol,
and the distinction between demonstrated capability and speculation. She does
not run manufacture, capital, or a national undertaking. Illness increasingly
limits presence, so later participation is principally through dated letters
and signed documents.

Her signature has force because the subscriber trust voluntarily makes it a
condition of the test and of published claims, not because she receives general
legal or financial control. William King-Noel retains the household role that
shapes her travel and available time without being made a venture principal.

Her growth is from believing that exact conditions can discipline collaboration
and that personally secured authority will preserve its function, to seeing
that conditions also require a funded human carrier and succession. In 1844 she
declines to name a deputy because a substitute chosen by the surrounding men
could empty her exceptional authority while appearing to preserve it. Illness
later makes the other danger visible: authority that only she can exercise is
also fragile. Her final capacity warning is therefore neither supernatural
foresight nor infallibility; it follows from the office's own hours and
obligations and corrects a one-carrier weakness she can now see in herself.

### Edward Halden

Explicitly fictional person, born in London in 1803 and formed as a clerk,
quantity estimator, and works agent on instrument and railway contracts. He is
married, supports two children, and has neither inherited wealth nor mechanical
genius. His pre-1843 background changes no documentary event.

Halden reads drawings as obligations: quantities, suppliers, interfaces,
inspection stages, payment stops, and consequences of accepted changes. George
Farrow, who knows Halden through instrument and railway supply and Babbage
through the former Clement network, forwards the inquiry after the divergence.
Halden first accepts a paid four-week estimate, not the Engine. He
earns the role of commercial founder only when his account makes refusal as
clear as continuation.

His strength is finite framing. His weakness is a desire for organizing work to
be recognized as making. The 1851 success therefore tempts him to convert
attention into contracts before he has converted it into checking capacity.
His growth occurs when he records his own authorization of that expansion and
closes the office rather than concealing its insolvency.

## Places and presence

The spatial model stays coarse because finer geometry would not change the
story.

A place is a persistent Thing with a lifecycle. A person's occupancy of it is
a presence Event; weather, traffic, work, or another changing condition of the
place may be opened as Events within that lifecycle when they alter access,
timing, choice, or consequence. Rendering may draw sensory detail from the
participating Things, their current activities, and any overlapping place
Events. Incidental atmosphere does not acquire causal force merely by appearing
in prose.

- **London** contains Babbage's working rooms, Halden's office, Farrow Precision
  Works, Guardian's office, and later the Calculation Office.
- **Ockham** is Lovelace's principal location during the August 1852 succession
  exchange. Her final November location is Great Cumberland Place in
  Marylebone. Letters and delivered packets are explicit communication Events
  when a decision depends on them.
- **Manchester** contains Whitworth's works. The 1844 review travels as drawings,
  gauges, and a written report; no scene assumes all principals were present.
- The Bounded Card Apparatus is present at Farrow's works during manufacture,
  at the Calculation Office from the 1851 test through closure, and in storage
  after dismantling. Its documents follow a separate custody chain.

No interaction requiring physical co-presence is used unless the participating
people share London at that time. Otherwise a letter, report, or messenger is
part of the event.

## The bounded apparatus

The apparatus is deliberately less than the Analytical Engine. It has six
signed six-decimal-digit registers, performs addition and subtraction, and
reads a prepared sequence of operation and variable cards of at most thirty
steps. A fixed block can be repeated after manual reset. Inputs and outputs are
manually transcribed. It has no stored conditional branch, automatic printing,
or claim to general-purpose operation.

The test question is exact: can this frozen apparatus execute the declared
card sequence on twelve sealed actuarial recurrence cases and reproduce the
independently calculated results on two complete runs? Passing this question
does not establish speed, economy, broad reliability, recurring service
capacity, or completion of the Analytical Engine.

### Office capacity known before expansion

One verified job packet requires approximately 120 preparer-hours and 40
independent checker-hours. The office promises 80 protected checker-hours per
quarter, enough for **two verified jobs**, not four. These are work estimates,
not elapsed times from deposit to delivery.

In February 1852 Halden accepts four staged contracts with final deliveries in
April--June 1854. The early stages use specimen figures to prepare methods and
card sequences. Customers close their accounts at the end of 1853 and reconcile
them during January--March 1854. Final signed returns suitable for independent
comparison are released no earlier than 1 April 1854. Guardian's arrives on
1 April; the other three releases remain at April resolution. Earlier work
uses specimens or provisional figures, whose checks cannot certify the final
signed sources: each completed packet still needs its 40 independent hours.
All four final checks therefore fall in April--June and require 160 hours in a
quarter for which only 80 are promised. This subsequent authored refinement
supersedes the earlier first-quarter final-source arrival: it closes the
otherwise available extra quarter of checking without adding a historical
claim, customer, or different capacity figure.

Babbage and Halden expect the long lead time to permit training and improved
methods, then defer funding the independent succession. Early preparation and
correction continue, but their completed work does not remove the final check.
The bottleneck is known before Lovelace's death and the eventual error; it does
not mean that four unchanged forty-hour tasks remain untouched for two years.

## Relationship processes

Relationships are joint Events plus each person's own readings and decisions;
they are not one scalar called trust.

### Babbage and Lovelace

- World process: active intellectual partnership, 1833--1852.
- Babbage repeatedly relies on Lovelace to express operational implications but
  resists letting that reliance become authority over scope.
- Lovelace relies on Babbage inside architecture but increasingly requires
  independent records for claims about completion and capacity.
- The completed trial gives them a brief shared delight in seeing an operation
  survive translation into matter. Their later disagreement costs something
  because neither was excluded from that joy.
- Their disagreement after the trial is represented by their different office
  Direction Cuts and by the signed qualification, not by opposing trust scores.

### Babbage and Halden

- World process: feasibility and venture partnership, 1843--1855; later
  interpretation continues separately in Babbage's life.
- Babbage relies on Halden's stage gates while they make material progress.
- Halden relies on Babbage's judgment only inside the frozen configuration;
  proposed improvements require a new decision.
- At the trial, Babbage experiences architecture as vindicated while Halden
  experiences organized delivery as creative work made visible. Their shared
  pleasure and the same later inquiries move both judgments.
- Success therefore makes each mistakenly treat the other's confidence as
  independent confirmation. Their aligned expansion preferences are a coupling
  event, not evidence that either possessed better information.

### Lovelace and Halden

- World process: evidential and contractual cooperation, 1843--1852.
- Each initially values the other's insistence on explicit conditions.
- They share the trial's success even while interpreting its consequences
  differently; Lovelace's qualification is a refusal to enlarge a result she
  genuinely values, not detachment from Halden's achievement.
- Their split concerns what follows from demand: Lovelace treats checking labor
  as a prior boundary; Halden believes deposits can finance capacity after
  commitment.
- Her capacity qualification survives their disagreement and later becomes
  evidence in his decision to disclose the error.

## One concept model: a valid computational claim

This is the single deep-grounding protocol test for the construction. It does
not require every concept in the world to receive a canonical model.

The concept is itself a small Meaning Model with this temporal structure:

1. state the exact question and exclusions;
2. freeze the relevant configuration and inputs before observing the answer;
3. execute while retaining a trace;
4. compare against evidence independent of the tested route;
5. repeat under the same declared conditions;
6. publish no claim broader than the observations;
7. preserve a correction route if later evidence disagrees.

The stable Concept identifier for this grounding is
`concept.07r2.valid-computational-claim`. Its canonical process Event is
`event.canonical.07r2.valid-computational-claim`, in a canonical-model context
under the constructor's understanding authority rather than accepted story

asserted historical dates or invented phase durations.
