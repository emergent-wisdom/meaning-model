# World data used by this construction

This file separates literal quantities in the accepted world from sibling-relative
Meaning Model weights. A literal quantity has an external unit or count and remains
meaningful when viewed alone. A semantic number appears only inside a named Cut,
beside all of its siblings and its remainder.

## Quantities to use now

| Family | Unit and examples | Record location | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time | calendar date; days, weeks, months, years | lifecycle, period, episode, contract, manufacture, travel, and delivery Events | Age and sequential shares are derived from dates and durations. |
| Money | nominal pounds sterling at a stated date and accounting boundary | private means, construction trust, household, and calculation-office processes; payment and liability Events | Keep distinct ledgers. Receipts, expenditures, debt, refunds, and closing cash must reconcile. Do not convert to present-day pounds inside the world. |
| Labor and service capacity | people, roles, person-hours per job, checker-hours per quarter, concurrent jobs, backlog | work, manufacture, and calculation-office Events | Capacity must name the constrained activity. Do not store generic human capacity as one scalar. |
| Machine configuration | registers, decimal places, cards or instructions, components and assemblies | Engine and configuration Events | Record only configuration facts that constrain manufacture, testing, or use. |
| Testing and error | cases, runs, checks, discrepancies, interventions, input and output rows | trial, operation, checking, and correction Events | Keep mechanism, transcription, procedure, and interpretation errors distinct. |
| Manufacture | component and assembly counts, labor and rework time, cost, inventory, delivery time; a declared length or tolerance where needed | manufacture, inspection, failure, and repair Events | Exact dimensions or tolerances are opened only where a scene or acceptance rule depends on them. |
| Place and movement | named place, presence interval, travel time; distance only when consequential | presence, access, delivery, and travel Events | Named containment is enough unless geometry changes what can happen. |
| Documents and information | delivery date, pages, tables, cards, entries, copies, revisions, turnaround time | document lifecycle, custody, access, comparison, and publication Events | Status, authority, and interpretation remain categorical or relational rather than numerical. |

The present counterfactual uses several authored quantities: the £25 estimate,
£3,000 staged construction capital, the £1,620 office ledger, four £180 customer
deposits and one £180 refund, a six-register apparatus, twelve sealed cases and
two runs, and two independently checked jobs per quarter against four accepted
jobs. These are construction commitments, not historical facts. They may be
revised together if finer work exposes an inconsistency, but every affected
ledger and consequence must move with the revision.

The four final deliveries fall in April--June 1854.
The accepted source window fixes the dependency: customers close their
accounts at the end of 1853, reconcile them through March 1854, and release
final signed returns suitable for comparison no earlier than 1 April. Guardian's
arrives on 1 April; the other three dates remain unresolved within April.
Earlier checks use specimens or provisional figures and cannot certify those
final signed sources. All four final comparisons therefore fall in April--June:
4 packets × 40 final checker-hours = 160 hours demanded against 80 promised in
that quarter. These dates are counterfactual world commitments;
the hours and derived shortfall do not change any Cut,
person profile, or financial account.

## Quantities to open only if needed

Machine setup and run time, throughput, component dimensions, interface tolerance,
material mass, fuel or power, travel distance, print quantity, and detailed wage or
rent rates are permitted. They are not filled merely because the engine can store
them. Each needs a unit, interval, uncertainty, and provenance or an explicit
`authored counterfactual` status.

## Not literal world numbers

Trust, standing, intelligence, kindness, relationship quality, meaning, fear,
importance, generic shock magnitude, and generic vitality are not assigned an
absolute scalar. They are represented by Events and evidence, or by a Cut whose
question supplies one comparative unit. Human effort may be recorded literally as
hours or money; its allocation across concerns may be a Cut. Health may use dated
illness facts and supported physical measurements; the optional
healthy-functioning/illness-burden/remainder profile is a comparative Cut, not a
clinical measurement.

The practical test is simple: if the number can be read without its siblings and
still names a physical, temporal, monetary, or counted quantity, it is world data.
If its meaning is “more of this than those alternatives,” it belongs to a Cut.
