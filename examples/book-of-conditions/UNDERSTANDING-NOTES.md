# Understanding notes for the editable world model

These are concise records of consequential authoring judgments. They are not
hidden reasoning transcripts, historical evidence, or story passages. Each
note points to the world decision it explains and may be superseded when the
world is revised.

## U01 — Why a third principal exists

Targets: E02--E06; Edward Halden's life.

Babbage and Lovelace can continue a negotiation but cannot, between them,
supply independent manufacture, commercial staging, and liability. The third
principal therefore carries the narrow integration function that makes the
divergence causal. He is fictional because assigning all of that function to a
known historical figure would distort the evidence. He does not absorb the
manufacturer, customer, financier, or successor roles.

## U02 — Why the machine is small

Targets: the Bounded Card Apparatus; E03, E06, E14.

A completed general Analytical Engine by 1851 would require far more
counterfactual industrial change than the story needs. A six-register,
addition-and-subtraction, card-directed apparatus gives the founders something
real to finish and test while preserving the distinction between the bounded
artifact and Babbage's architecture. If later technical opening shows even this
configuration to be implausible, reduce its registers or card sequence rather
than enlarging the industrial world to rescue it.

## U03 — Why success is the central shock

Targets: E14--E18.

Another mechanical failure would merely confirm every existing caution. A
bounded success changes the principals' wants and interpretations in different
directions and creates the temptation the conditions were designed to resist.
It therefore produces both stronger causality and a better story: their method
works, then they misstate what its success licenses.

## U04 — Why the office fails outside the mechanism

Targets: E17--E27.

The tested arithmetic should remain true. The office fails because recurring
calculation is a human-machine process containing preparation, checking,
delivery, correction, and money. The known two-job checking capacity, the
four-job commitment, the specific transposition, and the omitted duplicate
check make the failure traceable without making the apparatus magically break
at the required dramatic moment.

## U05 — Why Lovelace's death is not the cause of the error

Targets: E19--E24.

Her illness and death expose a succession failure already visible in the 1852
capacity review. The surviving principals choose not to fund a second checker
before she dies. Death removes her active pressure; it does not enter a wrong
number or force Halden's later delivery decision. This preserves agency and
avoids using a woman's death as a mechanical plot device.

## U06 — Why the ending is documentary

Targets: E27--E30.

The counterfactual earns one bounded proof, not an industrial revolution. The
complete packet is a meaningful surviving object because it contains success
and failure under the same identity. Babbage's late growth is the decision not
to curate away the parts that limit his preferred interpretation.

## U07 — Revision from the first candidate round

Targets: the whole 07r2 construction.

The earlier candidate passes showed that a plausible macro outcome was not enough:
continued negotiation lacked a person capable of converting it into priced,
staged obligations. This version retains the useful bounded-proof and
overcommitment pattern, adds the missing commercial founder, and narrows the
long-run outcome. Discarded casts, signature objects, prose, and numerical
worlds are not part of this construction.

## U08 — Pragmatic revision rule

Targets: all later openings.

When a finer event is uncertain but does not change the parent account, keep it
coarse or put it in the remainder. When a local route is weak, repair that
route. When the children reveal that the parent could not have happened, or
that the only coherent realization is dramatically inert, revise the parent
and propagate the change. Do not restart a whole world to avoid making an
ordinary authorial choice.

## U09 — Why this world is accepted

Targets: the complete macro, E01--E30, and the twelve-passage route.

Progressive opening found local defects—the access route to Halden, the formal
authority behind the 1852 contracts, and the exact 1854 delivery choice—but no
failure of the parent history. Farrow now supplies the access route, Halden
alone binds the customers after Babbage urges expansion, and the omitted check
has its own Direction Cut. The ledgers close, the prospective test remains
valid, and each principal's late action grows out of an earlier pattern. The
world is therefore accepted at its declared resolution. Unopened workshop,
subscriber, and customer detail stays remainder rather than delaying the book.

## U10 — Why assurance and threat are separated from fear

Targets: the principal period profiles; selected-scene WHAT and FEELS Cuts;
passages I--XII.

The five core wants identify what is receiving motivational attention. They do
not by themselves say whether the person expects those wanted endpoints to be
secured. The added outlook Cut therefore divides one comparable unit between
assured fulfillment, threatened fulfillment, and unresolved expectation; its
threat child may then be opened across the five wanted regions. Fear remains a
separate emotional appraisal because the same represented threat can instead
organize anger, sadness, bargaining, or action. This distinction guided a
small rendering revision: Babbage classifies mechanisms under threat,
Lovelace converts uncertainty into explicit conditions, and Halden moves from
bounded accounting through promotional accounting to self-audit. No world
event or causal dependency changed.

## U11 — Why the emotional-depth pass adds residue rather than shocks

Targets: E09--E11, E14, E18--E28; passages II, V, VII--XII.

The accepted world already contains the required long perturbations: earlier
institutional rupture makes finite dependence threatening to Babbage; bounded
success destabilizes both him and Halden; Lovelace's foreseeable absence tests
whether authority has a carrier; and the returned table forces a slow change in
what counts as the work's complete record. Adding another catastrophe would
weaken that causality. The revision instead repeats existing objects with
changed use—drawings cover ledgers, deposit letters cover the capacity line,
handwriting widens, checking tools wear, and proof and failure remain under one
tape. Explanatory prose was removed where those residues could show adaptation.

## U12 — Why character refinements change the model before the rendering

Targets: E04, E06, E09, E14, E17, E20, and E26; passages II--XII.

Lovelace's fallibility and the secondary people's lives need consequences beyond
their assigned jobs. The smallest correction does not add a new shock. It opens consequences
already latent in accepted Events. In the finite compact Lovelace declines an
unspecified deputy because substitution could dissolve the rare authority she
has won; in 1852 she recognizes that this protection also made her a single
carrier, which sharpens rather than magically supplies her succession argument.
Davies's curiosity makes evidential restraint a choice, Farrow protects his
foreman's finding despite delayed payment, and Neale tries to teach Wills
without counting supervised practice as independent capacity, then shares
responsibility for the correction. These refinements were entered in the life
and Event descriptions before their passages were revised. They do not alter
the macro outcome or any existing Cut composition. The remaining changes remove
balanced, thesis-bearing speech and replace it with handling, interruption, and
profession-specific language; those are rendering changes rather than new
world facts.

## U13 — Why invention joy is deepened

Targets: E03, E10, E14, E16, and E18; the finite-invention and
recognition-to-overreach change arcs; passages III, VI, VIII--X.

The rendering moves too quickly from making to governance.
The repair therefore opens experiences already causal in the accepted world.
Halden's reversal from pricing a machine part to pricing an answerable claim is
treated as a cognitive invention; the corrected assembly's first free motion is
allowed to reward several kinds of expertise; and the successful trial is
experienced as shared joy before commercial attention arrives.

The pleasure is not an ornamental scene. E14 changes beliefs about feasibility,
standing, and the value of each participant's work. E16 reinforces those
beliefs. In E18 Babbage and Halden then mistake two judgments moved by the same
success and response for independent confirmation. This was already the
recognition-to-overreach arc; the revision makes its positive shock perceptible.
That common-cause account belongs to the constructor's model and is not given to
Lovelace as an explicit diagnosis in the rendered scene. She can demonstrate
the capacity error without possessing the whole explanation of either man.
Accordingly, only the three immediate post-trial FEELS Cuts change. Their joy
shares rise and their anticipatory shares fall because the named moment is now
fixed after the second complete run and before the first inquiry. WHAT, HOW,
fulfillment outlook, threat, and Direction Cuts remain unchanged.

The same macro history and failure remain. The revision develops four literary
functions—cognitive discovery, pleasure in matter,
shared recognition, and intellectual intimacy under bodily cost—through the
current model's existing Events, relationships, Cuts, and change arcs.

## U14 — Why Lovelace discusses mechanical judgment

Targets: E20 and E20a; E23--E26 and E28; passage X; the valid-computational-claim
concept model.

Lovelace's published 1843 Notes already distinguish the Engine's ability to
follow supplied analytical relations from any power to originate those
relations, while also proposing that suitably represented relations outside
ordinary number—including harmony—could be subjected to operation. Passage X
does not quote the Notes or attribute modern terminology to her. It develops
those documented positions inside the counterfactual self-comparison design.

The exchange changes a record: a common-source fault causes Babbage to narrow
mechanical verification to comparison of internal operations. Lovelace states
only the distinction between an unforeseen consequence and intention.
Guardian's source sheet, followed by the returned table, supplies the adverse
case.

## U15 — Why voice and sensory detail are consequences of the model

Targets: principal life trajectories; E03, E06, E10, E14, E18, E20--E21, and E26;
passages I--XI.

The voice pass adds no Event, Cut, causal dependency, or outcome. Sentence
movement and diction are derived from the person's existing organization:
Babbage expands from the local question toward architecture, Lovelace translates
between representations and grows terser as bodily capacity declines, and
Halden names hours, money, workers, dates, and deliverables. Secondary voices
use the narrower evidence their work makes available. These are rendering
constraints recorded in `PERSON-MODELS.md`, not new personality coordinates.

Sensory detail is likewise rendered from Things already participating in an
Event and from their changing state: coupled assemblies transmit load, heat,
and vibration; the trial exposes cards, register faces, the slowing shaft, and
the sealed comparison case; the succession episode exposes drawings, breath,
packets, and the cold apparatus. A city or room remains a Place Thing; presence
and any consequential weather, traffic, work, or crowd condition are Events in
its lifecycle. A separate place Event is opened only if that condition changes
access, timing, choice, or consequence. This keeps atmosphere available without
letting an unmodeled detail become a hidden cause.

The final bounded prose pass opens the already recorded deputy refusal and
its later recognition, without adding a successor or changing the plot.
Lovelace hesitates before striking the provision; in illness she asks William
about the signature and admits she would have refused his substitution.
Recognition does not settle her attachment to the authority she secured.
Several narrator explanations are removed where the action already supplies
their meaning, and a few exchanges lose their immediate polished rebuttals.
E06, E20, the person description, and the route retain those same choices.
The Cut weights, accounting, dates, capacity, outcomes, and ending are
unchanged. A fresh retrospective import is required to bind the revised
descriptions and manuscript to their new source identities.

## U16 - Connect preparation to the final checking window

Targets: E09, E17--E18, E22--E24; passages VI, IX, and X.

A fresh complete-manuscript reading identified a missing link: at forty hours
per packet, four checks appear finishable long before the 1854 delivery unless
the reader knows why the final inputs are not yet available. The world model
already placed all four checks in one delivery quarter. This pass opens that
commitment: methods and specimen work precede customer returns closed at the
end of 1853; final figures arrive in early 1854 for April--June delivery.
Earlier checking cannot certify those new values. This is an authored source
dependency, not a fifth customer, new historical evidence, or new work invented
to consume every intervening hour.

Halden's error remains a gamble on future capacity followed by a refusal to
fund it. The existing 80-hour capacity, 40-hour final check, four contracts,
cash accounts, and decision Cuts are unchanged. The model descriptions and
loadable scaffold request are updated before the prose. The scene then shows
old checking marks being set aside instead of adding a speech about scheduling.

The same reading caught two consecutive Babbage speeches that looked like
alternating speakers in VI. His claim that the failure justifies the larger
redesign is joined into one attributed speech. No character position changes.
The witnessed test, emotional palette, death, closure, and custody ending are
preserved. This pass does not claim a fresh Rust import.

## U17 - Retrospective Rust import and the final-source cutoff

Targets: E17--E24; passages IX--X; the concept component Cut and person scaffolds.

The construction review distinguished a coherent authored artifact from a
complete native execution record. This repair imports the existing descriptions,
person processes, Cuts, and manuscript into a new database. It records the
source digests and new compilation and retrieval results; it does not recover
earlier editing order. The concept allocation gains an explicit zero remainder
and component addresses. All thirteen declared person profiles now request
their nine concurrent processes. Slow outlook remains authored: unselected
scenes are not invented to manufacture a temporal-mixture proof.

Checking the source schedule exposed a remaining causal weakness in U16's
refinement. Returns available during the first quarter could consume that
quarter's checking hours before April--June. The revised authored arrangement
is that customers reconcile the year-end returns during the first quarter,
then release the final signed checking copies from 1 April 1854. Guardian's
copy arrives on that date; other April arrivals remain coarse. Work on earlier
specimens or provisional figures cannot replace comparison against these final
copies. E22's window extends through April, and the model, route, and two
affected passages carry the same constraint.

This supersedes U16's source-availability assumption, not its preserved account
of the earlier revision. The four jobs, forty hours per final check, eighty
protected quarter-hours, omission on 7 April, refund, closure, and ending do
not change. Nor do the psychological weights. The alteration is a local repair
to the causal explanation, not new historical evidence or a rerolled world.
