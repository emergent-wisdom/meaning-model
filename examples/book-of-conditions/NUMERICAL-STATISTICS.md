# Numerical portrait of the accepted construction

Status: derived source inventory of the accepted `07r2` construction.
It introduces no new character judgment or story event. The canonical
component allocation is an explicit eight-answer Cut. The current manuscript
has 11,046 whitespace-delimited words. Source declarations and the compiler
request are counted here; the retained release receipt records the successful
retrospective Rust import.

## Reading rule

The construction contains two represented kinds of number and one derived
diagnostic kind.

1. **Cut weights** are sibling-relative shares of one explicitly named unit.
   They may be compared only when the question and children match. Every Cut
   includes a remainder and sums to one.
2. **World data** carry literal units such as pounds, hours, days, cases, card
   steps, and register positions. They follow the ordinary arithmetic of those
   units and are never mixed with Cut weights.
3. **Operational diagnostics** such as counts, density, total variation, means,
   ranges, and validator totals are calculated from the first two kinds. They
   describe or check the construction; they are not additional facts in its
   represented world.

There is no grand person score, global average across Cut families, or
statistical population claim in this report. The values are authored
descriptions of one constructed world.

### The bottleneck

- One verified packet requires 120 preparer-hours and 40 independent
  checker-hours: 75% preparation and 25% independent checking by role-hour.
- The quarter has 80 protected checker-hours, enough for 2 verified jobs.
- Four accepted jobs require 160 checker-hours in the delivery quarter.
- The demand/capacity ratio is **2.0×**: a shortfall of 80 checker-hours, or two
  fully checked jobs.

The office is therefore committed at 100% above verified checking capacity
before Lovelace's death and before the transposition occurs.

## Suitable claims for the papers

The current construction can support these descriptive statements:

- the construction recorded more than one hundred explicit compositional
  judgments while keeping every remainder visible;
- the accepted model assigns the same positive Event closely aligned immediate
  appraisals and sharply divergent later decisions;
- the four selected scene transitions move farther than the eight listed slow
  life transitions; this is a descriptive comparison of compatible authored
  Cuts, not a general law;
- the accepted model records four commitments against capacity for two, an
  80-hour checking deficit, and coordinated model and passage records; notes
  report model-before-prose editing, but same-commit Git snapshots do not
  independently establish the within-commit order;
- duration-derived composition, authored psychological composition, and
  real-unit accounting coexist without being confused.

It cannot yet support claims of predictive validity, inter-rater reliability,
population psychology, causal identification, or superiority over an
unstructured writing process. The preregistered literary comparison and
read-back experiment belong to the Meaning Model paper; predictive process-law
and real-data experiments belong to Life Simulation.
