# Principal life models

Status: accepted first-construction character models.

The nine slow views below are coordinates on a life, not siblings in a Cut and
not scores of human worth: body, kin, partnership, work, place, means,
knowledge, standing, and meaning. Only changes that matter to this story are
opened. Wants and decisions use Cuts only where one unit of attention or one
set of mutually exclusive continuations is actually divided.

The complete-life accounts remain qualitative because the nine concurrent
`IS` processes are not one quantity and must not be normalized against one
another. Two period-level Cuts below do carry numbers: fulfillment outlook
divides one represented unit among assurance, threat, and unresolved remainder;
when threat is opened, threat character divides that branch among the five
replaceable core wants. `SCENE-PROFILE-CUTS.md` supplies the shorter WHAT, HOW,
FEELS, and health Cuts at selected scenes.

## Whole lives first

These are the complete macro lives that precede scene construction. For
Babbage and Lovelace, the periods before 18 August 1843 are documentary history
at this resolution; later periods contain the explicit counterfactual. Halden's
entire life is authored fiction compatible with the era. Each person's periods
are disjoint and cover the life. The headings are interpretations, not claims
that human development obeys a universal stage vocabulary.

## Charles Babbage

### Whole-life pattern

Babbage repeatedly turns threatened dependence into greater architectural
generality and personal control. This response is genuinely intelligent when
the problem is conceptual and damaging when a finite institution must coordinate
other people's authority. His counterfactual growth is not that he ceases to be
Babbage. It is that he eventually treats a record of limitation as part of the
work rather than as an insult to it.

### Opened story periods

| Period | Slow changes that matter |
|---|---|
| 1833--Aug 1843: defense through abstraction | **Work:** material progress collapses while the Analytical Engine expands in design. **Means:** private support remains possible but government support ends. **Partnership:** the Clement rupture makes external control feel dangerous; Lovelace becomes an unusually capable interpreter. **Standing:** public refusal sharpens his need for recognition. **Meaning:** preserving the general architecture becomes entangled with preserving himself as its sole adequate judge. |
| Aug 1843--May 1851: bounded cooperation | **Partnership:** he permits Lovelace and Halden to hold real but delimited authority. **Knowledge:** manufacture teaches that individually correct parts can fail in composition. **Work:** one finite apparatus becomes materially real while successor designs remain open. **Meaning:** completion briefly means proving one exact claim rather than embodying the whole vision. |
| May 1851--May 1854: recognition and regression | **Standing:** the trial supplies long-denied public evidence. **Partnership:** he and Halden become mutually confirming after success; Lovelace's limiting reading feels obstructive. **Work:** demonstrated capability is rhetorically expanded into recurring service. **Meaning:** visible use again becomes a proxy for recognition of the whole architecture. |
| May 1854--Oct 1871: evidential restraint | **Partnership:** Lovelace is gone and the venture closes; his relation to both survives through documents and interpretation. **Knowledge:** he first separates the human error from the Engine, then accepts that the delivered computational system included preparation and checking. **Work:** general design continues without a claimed completed Engine. **Meaning:** the complete record becomes a more truthful continuation than a curated triumph. |

### Growth-bearing decisions

1. **1843:** tolerates a negotiation whose outcome he does not control.
2. **1844:** accepts a frozen production configuration while retaining a
   separate successor register.
3. **1852:** regresses by supporting four jobs against known checking capacity.
4. **1854:** initially narrows failure to human misuse.
5. **1871:** reverses that narrowing by preserving plans, proof, warning, error,
   and accounts under one custody identity.

### Conditional intelligence

When the problem is architectural, Babbage frames abstractions and implications
better than anyone around him. When the problem makes another person's
authority a condition of his own success, he tends to substitute a proxy
problem—whether the other person is diminishing the Engine—for the actual
problem of shared governance. The story must show both patterns in action; it
must not label him globally intelligent or unintelligent.

### Reliance rather than scalar trust

- Relies on Lovelace to interpret operations and implications.
- Does not willingly rely on her to define the final scope of his architecture.
- Relies on Halden to make stages and prices credible while progress is scarce.
- Stops treating Halden's caution as independent evidence after the trial,
  because both men now want expansion.
- By 1871 relies on the custody packet to say what no surviving collaborator
  can say for him.

## Ada Lovelace

### Whole-life pattern

Lovelace tries to convert intellectual insight into structures other people
must answer. At first she places too much hope in exact language and in her own
signature as the safest carrier of authority that others might dilute. The
counterfactual gives her a real success—the conditions enable a valid test—and
then lets her discover their limit: a rule without a funded successor can be
known, signed, and still not be performed.

### Opened story periods

| Period | Slow changes that matter |
|---|---|
| 1842--Aug 1843: interpretation becomes claim | **Work:** translation grows into the Notes and a governance proposal. **Knowledge:** she sees that the machine's importance lies in operations and representation, not metal alone. **Partnership:** intellectual dependence on Babbage coexists with resistance to being merely his expositor. **Meaning:** exact description appears able to create durable agency. |
| Aug 1843--May 1851: conditions become material | **Work:** test cases, exclusions, change records, and comparison rules enter the undertaking. **Standing:** her public authority remains constrained, but her conditions gain practical force inside the venture; she refuses an unspecified deputy for the claim signature because substitution could erase that authority. **Partnership:** she relies on Halden to make conditions costly and on Babbage to keep the architecture truthful. **Body:** illness periodically narrows presence and pace. |
| May 1851--Nov 1852: capacity and succession | **Knowledge:** she distinguishes proof of a frozen operation from proof of a service, and unforeseen mechanical consequence from judgment about premises and ends. She recognizes that she, like Neale, has been made a sole carrier. **Body:** declining health makes her own absence foreseeable. **Work:** attention moves from the successful test to checking capacity and succession. **Meaning:** continuity now requires a person, budget, and authority, not only a document or protected title. |

### Growth-bearing decisions

1. **1843:** asks for powers and conditions rather than praise or informal
   influence.
2. **1844:** accepts a smaller manufactured object without shrinking the
   conceptual architecture to it, but protects her claim authority through her
   sole signature rather than a succession rule.
3. **1846:** preserves the rejected improvement and the reason it was deferred.
4. **1852:** publicly qualifies a success she helped produce.
5. **1852:** recognizes the parallel between her own singular office and
   Neale's and leaves the unresolved request for checking succession in the
   record; the later common-source flaw in Babbage's self-comparison design
   sharpens her distinction between unforeseen consequence and judgment.

### Conditional intelligence

Lovelace is strongest at translating between representations: architecture,
operations, examples, claims, and consequences. Her recurring weakness is to
believe that a sufficiently exact document and a personally protected signature
can substitute for continuing power and labor. That error is understandable in
a world eager to absorb her authority into a man's. She grows when she
separates mechanical reach from the purposes and premises supplied to it,
recognizes that singular authority is also fragile, and identifies the missing
institutional carrier, even though she cannot make the others fund it.

That recognition does not make surrendering her own authority easy. At the
compact she hesitates over the deputy provision, then removes it; in illness
she can admit to William that she would have refused his substitution without
finding a satisfactory replacement. Her understanding of the problem runs
ahead of her willingness to accept its cost.

### Reliance rather than scalar trust

- Relies on Babbage for architectural truth and resists his inference from
  architectural possibility to present capability.
- Relies on Halden to enforce stages before success; withdraws reliance when he
  treats customer deposits as future checking capacity.
- Relies increasingly on signed records as her body limits presence, while
  learning that the records do not execute themselves.

## Edward Halden

### Whole-life pattern

Halden's life is organized around making other people's difficult work finite
and deliverable. Because such work is often treated as clerical rather than
creative, he is vulnerable to recognition once the apparatus succeeds. His
development is not from timid clerk to heroic founder. It is from disciplined
integration, through status-driven overcommitment, to owning the consequences
of the very exception he authorized.

### Opened story periods

| Period | Slow changes that matter |
|---|---|
| 1803--Sep 1843: competent but replaceable | **Kin:** marriage and two children make income continuity consequential. **Work:** clerking, estimating, supplier coordination, and contract administration build real competence without ownership. **Means:** modest savings, no independent construction capital. **Standing:** known inside contracting networks, largely invisible outside them. **Meaning:** usefulness is proved by making another person's design deliverable. |
| Sep 1843--May 1851: authority is earned | **Work:** the estimate becomes a staged venture; he gains bounded authority over cost, schedule, and accepted changes. **Knowledge:** material failure teaches him not to confuse correct drawings with a composed result. **Means:** salary and £100 capital expose his household without making him a magical financier. **Partnership:** Lovelace gives evidential form to his contractual instincts; Babbage gives the undertaking intellectual consequence. |
| May 1851--May 1854: recognition perturbs discipline | **Standing:** the successful trial makes him publicly visible as an organizer. **Means:** recurring fees appear to offer durable independence. **Work:** he begins treating signed deposits as a route to future capacity. **Partnership:** shared enthusiasm with Babbage weakens the independence of his judgment; Lovelace's caveat feels like refusal to let organization count as invention. |
| May 1854--1874: accountable limitation | **Standing:** disclosure and closure damage his reputation but distinguish failure from fraud. **Means:** his investment is lost; continued household support requires ordinary employment again. **Knowledge:** he learns that a gate enforced until prestige arrives is not yet a durable gate. **Meaning:** recording responsibility becomes the final form of the integrative work he wanted recognized. |

### Growth-bearing decisions

1. **1843:** accepts only a paid estimate and preserves the right to refuse.
2. **1844:** separates current configuration from successor changes.
3. **1846:** enforces rework despite Babbage's technically attractive redesign.
4. **1852:** violates his own capacity boundary and authorizes four jobs.
5. **1854:** authorizes delivery without the duplicate check.
6. **1854--55:** records his authorization, refunds the affected customer, and
   closes rather than finance concealment.

### Conditional intelligence

Halden is strongest when obligations are still hypothetical and can be made
finite. He becomes weaker when prior success and recognition turn refusal into
a threat to his identity. The later disclosure shows adaptation: he applies
the same ledger intelligence to his own failure rather than only to other
people's projects.

### Reliance rather than scalar trust

- Relies on Babbage for architecture inside the frozen configuration, not for
  unilateral changes to it.
- Relies on Lovelace for the exact boundary between observation and claim.
- Mistakes Babbage's post-trial confidence for independent confirmation when it
  is partly caused by the same public response that changed his own judgment.
- After the error, relies on the written caveat he earlier discounted.

## Long shock and adaptation chains

These are links among existing Events, not a new profile or a fitted law. The
shock names the faster event; adaptation is the later pattern across the
person's disjoint life periods. The numerical evidence remains in the period
and scene Cuts below.

| Person | Anticipation and shock | Immediate response | Longer adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Babbage | The Clement rupture and government withdrawal make another finite dependency an anticipated threat. The 1851 success is a large positive shock, experienced first as joy and shared recognition; the 1854 returned table is its countershock. | He converts success into a claim for expansion, then protects the mechanism by placing the office outside it. | **Cascade, then slow recovery.** Threat moves from autonomy and competence toward the problem of understanding what the work includes. Between 1855 and 1871 he repeatedly keeps the successful and adverse papers together before he can fully state why. |
| Lovelace | Declining bodily capacity makes absence foreseeable. The four-job decision and rejected succession cost reveal two related vulnerabilities: her claim authority and Neale's independent checking each have one active carrier. | She recognizes that she once protected her office without providing for its continuity, qualifies the success, names the missing person, money, and refusal authority, and leaves the unanswered decision attached to the record. | **Anticipated and terminal transition.** Brief assurance after the trial does not consolidate. Her agency moves increasingly into letters and signed records; death ends her agency, while the records continue to affect later decisions without acting by themselves. |
| Halden | The 1847 credit contraction rehearses a choice between appearance and paid obligations. The 1851 success supplies joy and recognition he did not expect; the returned table exposes the exception that recognition helped him permit. | He mistakes confidence shared with Babbage after one success for independent confirmation, treats deposits as purchasable future capacity, then traces the failure through his own two authorizations. | **Cascade, then consolidation through repair.** He discloses, refunds, closes, returns to ordinary estimating, and later supplies the reconciled account. The later baseline retains threat to competence, understanding, and household well-being without making obscurity identical to failure. |

The rendering rule is residue rather than explanation. Babbage's drawings and
successor register, Lovelace's widening handwriting and chair, Halden's ledger
and initials, and the workers' ruler and checking strip recur before and after
the relevant shocks with changed use. This lets the prose show consolidation
across years while the model keeps belief, emotion, action, and world result as
separate Events.

## Three sparse scene-WHAT concern Cuts

These use the run's five replaceable concern concepts. Each is one local Cut of

## Character test for later scenes

Every selected scene must do at least one of these:

- expose a difference between architectural, evidential, or contractual
  intelligence;
- make one person's reliance on another consequential;
- show the same event producing different anticipation, appraisal, or
  adaptation;
- change a slow life process or reveal that an apparent change did not
  consolidate;
- force a choice between a deep aim and the proxy that once served it.

If a scene does none of them, it may belong in the world but not in the book.
