## Concepts
- concept.keeping (Keeping): What remains available while no one attends to it, judged by a stated observer over a stated interval. Used to explain Candelaria's confidence that what she loves will keep.
- concept.keeping.average (Keeping as an average): A long integration stays stable while every part of what it averages changes.
- concept.keeping.body (Keeping as a body): Physical persistence: a star, a planet or a mountain continues whether watched or not.
- concept.keeping.person (Keeping as a person): A person continues as the same person but does not wait unchanged; what is kept between people is kept only by continued attention.
- concept.keeping.record (Keeping as a record): A stored observation or message stays exactly as it was made; it does not change with its subject.
- opening abstract_cut.keeping.kinds: concept.keeping → concept.keeping.average, concept.keeping.body, concept.keeping.person, concept.keeping.record (Alternative kinds of persistence an observer may be relying on; overlapping readings, not shares of a physical total.)
