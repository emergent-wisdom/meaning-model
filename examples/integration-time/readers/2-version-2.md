## Problems to fix

1. **Food reserve.** "Eighty days" is wrong for two passengers on an eleven-month trip home, which needs about 670 person-days, unless they travel in torpor, and the bad bunks seem to rule that out. With about 670 person-days the pair have roughly 2.9 years of food against a ship due in four, so the gamble still works.
2. **Light level.** Full-Moon light is about 0.25 lux. The crisp hand-shadow and the lentil of sunlight on the counter need a dim tube and galley, and one clause would do it.
3. **Natural explanation.** Evenly spaced bands migrating at a steady rate are also what self-organised vegetation does on Earth (tiger bush). Candelaria should rule it out before writing "agent". That would also make "That they believe it" a real question.
4. **"Both".** Candelaria's reason for accepting it is narrated from outside ("and not like the answer, and choose it anyway"). Replace that with one spoken reason.
5. **Echoes.** The final section rhymes on nearly every beat: chair and crate, lentil, flat hands, "You both did", finished sentences. Only the "Send it now" reversal fully earns its place. Cut two echoes, starting with "the way Graciela's had been on the console".
