# Ideas for pieces in their voice

How to propose what a person could publish next, from a model of them. Start with
[PERSON-AND-VOICE.md](PERSON-AND-VOICE.md) (`life-sim://guide/person-and-voice`). This needs a voice model, a person
model or both; say which view the ideas come from.

## What an idea holds

Record each idea as a note of kind `idea` with the topic `ideas`, linked to the records it rests on:

- **the register and the audience,** and what that audience should think or do afterwards;
- **the angle:** what the piece says, in a sentence;
- **why it is theirs:** the drive, stance, turning point or recurring theme behind it, citing the person records; with
  the voice view, how they would say it, citing the voice records;
- **what it would use:** the moments and facts in the model that it needs;
- **what is missing:** facts or moments the model does not hold, as questions for them.

No drafts in this mode: an idea is chosen first, then written as a piece.

## Finding ideas

- Start from the person: what they care about now, what they are working toward, the tensions they live with, what
  recently happened to them, and the turning points they have not yet talked about publicly.
- Then the audience: what each audience wants from them, from their goals and their reach.
- Check each idea against what they have already published. A new angle is worth more than a reworded old post; an
  idea that only retells a published post says so.
- Never propose an idea that needs an experience, result or quote they have not described.

## Choosing

Give a handful of ideas per register, each in a few lines, and let the person choose. Record their choice and their
reasons as their report; a rejected idea and its reason teach the model as much as an accepted one.
