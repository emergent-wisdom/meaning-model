# Meaning Model paper structure

This document is the editorial map for `paper/meaning-model.tex`. It explains
the paper's argument and the boundaries checked by
`paper/meaning-model-structure.test.mjs`. It is not evidence that the grammar is
true, useful, or fully implemented.

## Status and objective

The manuscript is a theory and construction paper with a completed Book and a
partial descriptive construction witness. Its objective is to show how a model can
construct a world, abstract meanings, structurally situated understanding, and
a story together at progressive resolution. *The Book of Conditions* has been
completed at one declared resolution. Its artifact counts and local numerical
diagnostics are admissible descriptive evidence; the preregistered matched
comparison and independent read-back study remain prospective. No record,
scene, count, or result from a discarded earlier Book is admissible evidence.

The repository keeps a minimized grammar specification beside the explanatory
paper. The paper is self-contained: it explains the definitions and rules,
architectural argument, examples, completed construction account,
implementation boundary, descriptive results, and comparison plan. The minimized
grammar is a compact reference to the same account and adds no requirements.
Substantive changes must appear in both presentations, with the same core,
selected-profile, or non-normative status.

## Paper boundary

The Meaning Model owns:

- the six-form world grammar and the reasons for its distinctions;
- optional construction profiles, local Cuts, refinement, and revision;
- progressively opening Things, Events, relations, space, and concepts;
- ancestry-derived perspective projections and the integration of Understanding
  Nodes and Document Nodes with the world;
- frozen-parent whole candidates, direction rolls, atomic
  commit/reject/revise, rendering routes, and read-back; and
- the preparation, construction, release, and matched comparison of the new
  *Book of Conditions*.

Life Simulation uses completed or ongoing Meaning Model worlds. It owns
process-history generation, inference, learning, and empirical comparison;
compact temporal laws are optional. Real-data use, games, Ontology of the Alien
and Fractal Intelligence integrations, and alignment-facing curricula remain
there. Meaning Model construction and acceptance rules apply throughout, not
only before Life Simulation begins. The paper explains the interface without
reproducing the companion's methods.

## Argument sequence

### 1. Introduction

Experience is not already segmented into stable objects and events. A boundary
does not by itself preserve reference when the same person, machine, relation,
or episode is viewed at another resolution. The paper therefore seeks a small
grammar whose accepted identities and coarse commitments survive progressive
opening. Its claim is finite and constructive: recursive refinement and
sufficiency for a declared task and resolution, not a final ontology.

The introduction distinguishes an external semantic workspace used by a human
or AI from a candidate training language through which a learner acquires
compatible construction and revision practices. It does not require that
learned internal representations reproduce the record syntax. Meaning Model
construction and Life Simulation process methods operate together; the Book
exercises the workspace use without establishing the learning hypothesis.

The architectural claim concerns a complete authoring operation, not novel
record forms in isolation. The abstract leads with that operation and states
its prospective learning value rather than only listing the schema. The
introduction makes four changes explicit: detail constructed under coarse
commitments; coordinated revision across world, concepts, Event descriptions,
understanding, and prose; concept models in the grammar of their instances; and
question-relative numerical judgments rather than freestanding semantic scores.
Its example distinguishes revising an action from revising what the action
exemplifies. These are linked parts of the architecture, not four established
priority claims. Related Work compares DOC, DOME, Generative
Agents, and Narrative World Model directly, crediting hierarchical planning,
temporal graph memory, reflection, and revision. The proposed distinction is
their coordination through declared preservation/revision rules across world,
concepts, reasons, and prose. This is not a claim of proven priority or measured
literary, learning, or computational advantage. The comparison unit is the
complete authoring operation and its contracts; isolated antecedents neither
dispose of nor establish the novelty of that method. The conclusion returns to
learning how to choose and repair representations, leaving the training loop
and its tests to Life Simulation. No new grammar obligation follows from this
contribution framing.

### 2. Grammar and Progressive Construction

Six forms preserve distinctions that validators must treat differently:

- **Concept** — reusable meaning;
- **Thing** — continuing identity;
- **Event** — occurrence or persistence;
- **Binding** — a Thing in a time-scoped Event role;
- **Cut** — one declared unit divided under one question; and
- **Realization** — an unweighted grounding correspondence situated by a context parent.

Every Cut has an Event parent. A graded judgment about another record or
fragment therefore uses an assessment Event beneath the relevant perspective
process and linked `about` that target; semantic composition is never stored as
an Event payload. Perspective comes from authorized authority-parent paths,
not a repeated field. A Cut answer or remainder has an address consisting of
its exact Cut revision and local key. A nested Cut identifies the component
whose conditional unit it divides. Component addresses do not add world record
forms.

A relation among Things is a joint Event with one Binding per role. Typed links
such as `contains`, `about`, `updates`, and `supersedes` remain distinct. The
parsimony rule admits a new form only when typed fields cannot preserve a
validator-relevant difference in admissible targets, composition, authority,
inference, or validation.

#### Optional construction profile

The grammar specification labels every axiom as either a core obligation or a
selected-profile axiom. The core does not require one lifecycle convention or
psychology. The profile used in the paper adds lifecycle anchoring, three Cut
families, immutable revision, and five cross-record rules. Every semantic number is a
Cut weight relative to siblings under a named question and unit. The
answers and remainder sum to one. Unweighted Event-process links and externally
grounded Event data are not alternative kinds of Cut.

The six operation families are register, bind, open/compose, cut, decide, and
realize. Familiar names such as presence, membership, roll, roster, render, and
read-back specialize these records or surface rules rather than enlarging the
world grammar.

#### Progressive world construction

The Universe is a root Thing and History its lifecycle Event. Each Thing in the
profile has a lifecycle and declared continuity criterion. Relations,
membership, physical part--whole structure, and presence are Events with typed
Bindings; rosters and encapsulation are derived views. Places are Things, while
position and extent are externally grounded Event data when needed.

The externalization rule opens a record when it must persist, constrain a later
Event, differ across perspective processes, or remain controllable and auditable. Otherwise the
detail remains implicit. Only a governing Cut can retain unallocated share in
its remainder. This is the profile's defense against unbounded articulation.

The numerical boundary is explicit:

1. a Cut divides one unit and sums to one;
2. shorter Events may constitute or alter a longer Event-process without
   weights; and
3. dates, money, counts, locations, and physical measurements keep their own
   external units as Event data.

Allocation, sequential, and direction Cuts answer different questions.
Sequential shares derive from disjoint duration. Direction children are
possible continuations rather than current parts. Compatible fine Cuts
recompose through the mixture law; incompatible structures use their own
contracts or an explicit revision. A two-period numerical fixture checks the
mixture directly. A finite refinement witness declares protected queries,
observation times, resolution, tolerances, and a fine-to-coarse projection.
Close reconstructs the protected answers from fine records. Partial temporal
detail must leave a nonnegative residual of the remaining mass. Concept
specialization, expressive decomposition, physical encapsulation, and temporal
segmentation have different preservation contracts. Concurrent life processes may overlap and do not sum;
slow/fast names response time, not importance. Ordinary links do not explain a
longer process: explanation requires a situated assessment Cut or an
Understanding Node.

Anticipation, shocks, and adaptation are optional cross-scale readings of the
same Events and processes, not additional primitives or a universal cycle.
Prior expectations remain distinct from constructor proposal weights; missing
anticipation is unknown rather than zero. Growth and aging may be drift, and
response need not be recovery or improvement. Cross-scale links need explicit
explanations, not merely containment or a numerical mixture. Holling's panarchy,
Dalio's historical comparisons, and event-segmentation theory are related
accounts, not laws adopted by the grammar.

### 3. Concepts and Examples

A stable Concept identifier is distinct from a grounding version. Four
compatible resources are permitted: relational definitions, representative
prototypes, exemplar sets, and canonical Meaning Model fragments. A hashed
grounding bundle may be shallow or deep. The Realization records the exact
bundle used, context parent, evidence, and alignment; it is not itself a degree. A
graded judgment requires an assessment Event beneath the relevant perspective process about the target
and a local match/nonmatch/remainder Cut on that Event. Fit is distinct from
confidence in the evidence or interpretation; a numerical epistemic assessment
uses a separate Cut and never overwrites world state.

The section preserves two different histories. A **concept world** uses Things
and Events to record public carriers, formulation, publication, uptake,
revision, split, and decline. A **Concept-scoped Understanding Graph** follows
Understanding Nodes about grounding choices, evidence, rejected alternatives, and
superseding definitions. The love-and-Engine model is the single worked example
of optional canonical grounding and transparent fit arithmetic.

#### Worked examples

Earth and one evening of reading show crossing routes through one world without
duplicating identity. The avalanche shows physical direction without implying
a mind. The tendency–regulation–want progression shows how an optional actor
profile can add commitments gradually without making motivation a universal
primitive.

### 4. World, Understanding, and Story

The complete construction is one addressed graph with three semantic surfaces:
six-form world records, text-bearing Understanding Nodes, and ordered Document
Nodes. Shared
addresses permit exact reference but do not give the surfaces the same clock,
authority, or validation. Cognitive coverage can be empty, selective, or dense;
missing Understanding Nodes mean cognition was not externalized at that resolution.

Authority-parent ancestry keeps accepted world state, character belief, constructor
reasoning, and narrator disclosure apart. Temporal inclusion and reference do
not grant authority or access. Imagined and remembered content can have its
own represented time without being promoted to accepted public history.
Observation and communication records expose only their specified content at
the cutoff, not all state on the source record. A Concept-scoped reasoning history is
a projection across Understanding Nodes and exact grounding versions, not an
opaque field inside a Concept.

An actor's operative perspective projection is distinct from every observer's
estimate of it. Speech and action use the actor's model and accessible evidence.
Observer estimates are Events under the observer's inner process and linked
`about` their targets.
Categorical or externally measured estimates may use Event data; estimates of
semantic compositions use compatible Cuts on those assessment Events. Total variation or
categorical mismatch is a derived diagnostic, not another semantic scale.

Construction freezes the accepted parent and proposes a complete cross-surface
candidate. Rejection is side-effect free; acceptance is atomic; revision names
what it supersedes. Direction rolls permit the model to choose among visible
alternatives. A rendering route fixes source commit, epistemic status, event
route and frontier, narrator and viewpoint, evidence cutoff, disclosure policy,
and target story-passage Document Node. Read-back infers records from prose in a fresh context and
resolves mismatches through another explicit candidate. The succession example
uses the completed Book's E20, E21, E24, and E25, Understanding note U05, and
Chapters X and I. Its figure separates story chronology from the reverse
disclosure order and from construction-time explanation. Death does not force
the later omission. The notes and chapters were subsequently imported as native
surface records; that retrospective receipt does not recover the original
cross-surface authoring transactions.

### 5. Closure and Sufficiency

Closure is recursive use of the same forms, not the assertion that every
relation is a weighted tree. Resolution is declared: cells, people,
institutions, planets, and galaxies can use the same identity/lifecycle
scaffold while retaining domain-specific content. Wrapping any finite simulator
is cheap; discovering useful stable identities, cuts, and abstractions is the
substantive hypothesis.

Psychological resolution applies the same rule to replaceable person profiles.
An account of resistance to delegation can open into motives, beliefs, and
relationship histories without imposing one universal tree or normalizing
concurrent processes together. Same-question openings preserve coarse
commitments; new questions create separate views, and changed accounts require
explicit revision. More capable models may choose, deepen, or simplify these
representations. Meaning Model supplies the contract; Life Simulation owns
learning whether a refinement helps.

One application is a connected account of actual world history and geography.
Evidence-supported shared identities link sources without collapsing their
claims or disagreements. Spatial and temporal detail grows selectively, and
new evidence may require explicit revision; world-event time remains distinct
from the time the account changed. Acceptance is a supported modeling decision,
not authorship of reality. Fictional contexts remain separate. Meaning Model
supplies the representation and revision contracts; Life Simulation develops
corpus-wide construction, interpretation, and training through that interface.

The five tests are coverage, reference, grounding, recomposition, and value.

### 6. *The Book of Conditions*: Completed Book, Partial Witness

The Book is a completed bounded construction whose profile is explicitly
non-universal; its records supply a partial witness, not a fully native
end-to-end implementation. It uses 34 content-addressed flat Concept bundles, one later
unscored concept model, limited perspective projections, registered people,
places, institutions, documents, and machine parts, and only the Cut questions
and world data required at the opened frontier.

Its compact person profile distinguishes IS, WHAT, HOW, and FEELS. IS contains
nine concurrent unweighted life processes. WHAT opens concern, conditional
approach or threat avoidance, and AtomicWant leaves while keeping the separate
threat-belief--fear--action chain. HOW and FEELS use scene assessment Events
contained beneath the relevant life process. Scene profiles are elicited; compatible slower
profiles are duration-derived with coverage; conditional profiles remain
situation-specific. Every question and child vocabulary is replaceable.

Its optional shock view applies the general cross-scale account. A table traces
the historical credit constraint, succession and later custody, the interface
failure and rework, and the trial's stopped run, restart, and pleasure through
accepted Event IDs. Existing numerical figures show authored changes, not
measured recovery curves. These support representational coverage; Life
Simulation owns tests of jump-diffusion or other laws.

The actual construction froze preparation, registry, and historical prefix;
generated and rejected five insufficient whole-history candidates; then built
an authored pragmatic successor by expanding one macro paragraph to five,
repairing local defects, opening complete lives before scenes, and rendering a
twelve-unit route. No Book-level Direction roll selected the final world.
Meaning-changing revisions were entered into Event or life descriptions before
their passages changed; purely verbal revisions remained rendering choices.

The implementation witness distinguishes three layers. Rust compiled static
person, Thing, relationship, Concept, and change-arc scaffolds and supplied
narrow hashing, revision, persistence, and stale-parent transaction behavior.
An external validator checked the selected Cut, ancestry, lifecycle, presence,
access, and route contracts. Macro selection, local repair, judgments, and prose
remained authored conventions. Rust did not natively execute the complete Cut,
ancestry, context, cross-surface transaction, rendering, or read-back contract.

The descriptive portrait reports 34 Concept bundles, 106 Cuts, 464 child and
remainder weights, 30 primary plus seven opened Events, 13 complete lifecycles,
four macro trajectories with 13 phases, and 12 chapters containing 10,916
words. Cut weights, typed world data, and derived diagnostics remain distinct.
Counts, density, total variation, means, ranges, and ratios are operational
diagnostics rather than new Meaning Model state or population statistics.
Two figures expose compatible trajectories without introducing another scale:
slow period outlooks are stacked categorical interval blocks, and selected
local scene outlooks are chronological paths through separate magnified views
of their shared three-child simplex. Horizontal and vertical position give the
assured and threatened shares, diagonal guides expose the remainder, and curved
arrows show order while separating reversals. They encode neither duration nor
interpolation.

A separate repository-local reference bundle, `examples/refinement-trial/`,
illustrates selected contracts without the Rust host. Eleven executable test
families cover a synthetic apparatus trial, preserved and rejected openings,
partial residual feasibility, revision, and restricted access. One saved
fresh-context assistant extraction recovers explicit prose facts, not the
unspoken outlook weights. This is not a general parser, a validation of the
Book's historical claims, or the prospective independent read-back study.

The prospective comparison uses equal-information baselines and reports
structural conformance, contradictions, reader judgments, Cut elicitation and
optional direction calibration, total-variation and categorical read-back,
two-perspective preservation, appraisal and strategy consistency, density,
remainders, rejection rates, length, and all construction, inference, human,
and maintenance costs. The primary contrast, unit and sample size, violation
definitions, auditor adjudication and agreement, output floor, read-back
independence, tolerance calibration, and other thresholds await
preregistration. Negative findings are required. No comparative result is
assumed.

### 8. Limitations and Research Boundary

Limitations keep normalization separate from truth, declare vocabularies and
boundaries contestable, preserve the epistemic limits of perspective models, and
state that the Book must earn the cost of explicit structure.

The Life Simulation bridge explains an ongoing feedback loop: construct,
render, infer from available observations, compare predictions with later
evidence, and revise. Meaning Model read-back checks what prose says about a
world; Life Simulation tests hidden-process inference and transfer. Correction
can change a decomposition or concept grounding, not only fill missing detail.
Imagining the Corpus aligns text and tracks; Understanding Nodes preserve
attributed interpretation; Entangled Alignment supplies the recommended
Reader-based training approach, intended to inform the trained model's later
writing, dialogue, and action. Life Simulation additionally proposes learning
from documented world and scene-construction choices. That further training
source is distinct from using Reader-based learning when writing; neither
guarantees empathy or aligned conduct.

### 9. Conclusion

The conclusion returns to the practical value of a usable coarse world and the
longer-term hypothesis of learning to construct, interpret, and improve such
worlds. It retains the completed Book, partial construction witness, and
prospective comparative claim. Source checks permit up to 16,500 whitespace
tokens including TeX, tables, and appendices, accommodating the essential rules
previously delegated to the compact grammar as well as the reference example;
this ceiling is not a word count for the prose.

## Protected editorial invariants

The structure test must ensure that:

- the paper explains the essential rules itself and the minimized grammar
  provides a compact restatement, not additional requirements;
- the title and abstract describe worlds, concepts, understanding, and stories
  at progressive resolution;
- the manuscript admits the current construction witness but no discarded Book
  evidence or completed preregistered comparison;
- all six core forms and the local-simplex/remainder contract remain present;
- every grammar axiom is labeled core or selected-profile;
- every Cut has an Event parent; semantic estimates and graded fit use
  compatible Cuts on assessment Events beneath explicit perspective ancestry;
- every record query returns all typed parent-to-root paths, and every Cut query
  returns its complete sibling context rather than an isolated weight;
- Event-process links and world data are not normalized as Cuts;
- unmodeled detail remains implicit unless a governing Cut supplies a
  remainder;
- the life-process distinction, two-period mixture check, physical-part
  presence rule, and capacity/exchange boundary remain explicit;
- prototypes, exemplars, relational definitions, and canonical fragments all
  remain permitted;
- grounding hashes may identify flat bundles as well as canonical fragments;
- the externalization stopping rule and actor-versus-estimate distinction remain
  explicit;
- the actual-world history/geography application retains evidence, disagreement,
  separate fictional contexts, selective detail, and explicit revision rather
  than claiming complete knowledge or merging all sources into truth;
- Understanding Nodes and Document Nodes remain integrated but semantically
  distinct, while Event descriptions remain optional Event text;
- the cross-surface candidate, roll, rendering route, and read-back belong to
  the Meaning Model;
- the Book construction is complete, its profile remains non-universal, its
  pragmatic successor is not attributed to a Direction roll, and its matched
  comparison remains prospective;
- Rust scaffolds, external validation, and authored conventions remain
  explicitly separated;
- Cut weights, typed world data, and derived operational diagnostics remain
  distinct, and descriptive statistics make no causal, predictive, population,
  or superiority claim;
- the Book profile names IS/WHAT/HOW/FEELS, the separate appraisal chain,
  temporal derivation, conditionality, and its bounded concept histories;
- shock and adaptation remain optional representational views rather than
  assumed laws; and
- process-law discovery, process-aware learning, games, real-data use, and
  alignment curricula remain assigned to Life Simulation.

`paper/SPLIT-MIGRATION.md` is the loss-prevention ledger. If an important claim
leaves this paper, that file must name its destination before deletion.
