\caption{The construction loop. Left: what the shared instructions ask the
calling language model to do. Construction is the work: the model declares the
question and starts from its macro-processes, explores and discovers implicit
patterns and processes, named or not, makes each explicit, builds structures
that depend on the existing ones and interprets them, and refines their
trajectories. When it generates, draws and random words propose what it would
not have chosen where a decision or a trajectory has real alternatives, and it
keeps the most interesting with a recorded reason; when it describes, it asks
for data and infers what the evidence supports. It writes from the model's
state at each moment. Each round explores from and builds on the structure the
last one left. Review (dashed) comes now and then, from a blind read-back, the
director, another model, or a person, to find where the model is thin or
wrong; the model is then deepened or revised, and what was built on it, prose
included, is brought into line. The order is a guide rather than a fixed
sequence; any step may lead back to exploration. Middle: what deterministic
tool code does. It validates and stores every change, returns questions after
each one, records draws with their seeds, refuses gated operations until the
required reviews exist and their findings are answered, and lists the
dependents a revision puts in question. The estimator is optional, and the
scene and release gates and the seed-word and trajectory tools belong to the
storytelling add-on. Right: the one versioned record that every step reads and
changes through the tool. The tool checks records and their declared links; it
does not supply the interpretations or certify that an account is adequate.}
\label{fig:construction-loop}
